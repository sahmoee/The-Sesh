# The Sesh Support

Email [support@sowensstudios.com](mailto:support@sowensstudios.com) with the app version, device model, iOS version, and a concise description. Do not send session tokens, private journal exports, or sensitive photos unless support specifically requests a safe diagnostic.

For account deletion, use the subject **The Sesh Account Deletion** and include the handle and friend code visible in your profile. Sign out and disconnect Spotify from Privacy & Safety when appropriate. Immediate health or safety concerns should be directed to local emergency or medical services; The Sesh does not provide medical advice.
