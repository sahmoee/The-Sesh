Privacy Policy

Private journal information stays on your device and optional private iCloud. Information reaches our service only when you authenticate or use community, notification, Spotify, or other connected features.

Information we handle

The app stores profile settings, journal entries, cannabis session details, moods, effects, notes, stash records, goals, playlists, preferences, and photos you add. Community services may process an Apple or guest identifier, email or relay email, profile and friend code, session and DeviceCheck information, push token, friends, blocks, follows, presence, reports, statuses, shared session details, music activity, posts, media, comments, reactions, polls, and messages. Cloudflare may process IP address and operational request data.

Your sharing choices

Controls determine whether friends see activity, strain details, music, session duration, live status, and friend-code discoverability. Shared content can be saved or reshared by its audience. Spotify and Apple services process connected music and iCloud data under their policies.

Use and retention

Information supports authentication, sync, community, notifications, moderation, security, and support. We do not sell personal information, use ad SDKs, or track you across apps. Local and iCloud data remain until removed. Service records remain while needed for the service, safety, reports, security, or law; limited backups and security logs may persist temporarily.

Deletion

Remove local data and sign out in the app. For server-held account and community deletion, email support@sowensstudios.com with your handle and friend code. We may retain limited safety, fraud-prevention, or legally required records.

Age, safety, and security

The Sesh is only for adults age 21 or the legal age where they live and is not directed to children. Cannabis information is sensitive. We use HTTPS, signed sessions, Keychain, access controls, and rate limits, but no system is completely secure.

Questions: support@sowensstudios.com. Read the complete repository policy.
