## Description

Make every session more intentional.

The Sesh is a private journal, companion, and optional community for adults of legal age in places where cannabis is legal. Remember what worked, notice patterns over time, and choose exactly what—if anything—you share.

JOURNAL THE DETAILS
Log sessions, strains, methods, moods, effects, notes, photos, and reflections in a polished timeline designed around quick entry.

SEE YOUR PATTERNS
Turn your history into useful recaps, trends, cost insights, tolerance planning, stash forecasts, and personal milestones. Insights are informational and are not medical advice.

BUILD BETTER ROUTINES
Use pacing tools, hydration reminders, goals, tolerance-break planning, and responsible-use guidance. The Sesh never sells products or connects you with sellers.

REMEMBER THE SOUNDTRACK
Connect supported music services to capture what was playing, build playlists, and revisit the songs associated with a moment.

CONNECT ON YOUR TERMS
Join text-based community spaces, friends, chats, and shared sessions when you want them. Granular privacy controls let you decide whether to share activity, strain details, music, duration, and live status. Block and report tools are built in.

PRIVATE BY DEFAULT
Personal journal information stays on your device and, when enabled, in your private iCloud. Community information is sent only when you use social features. There are no advertising SDKs and no cross-app tracking.

The Sesh is intended only for adults age 21 or the legal age where they live. It does not provide medical advice, diagnose conditions, facilitate cannabis purchases, or encourage unlawful use. Never drive or operate machinery while impaired.
