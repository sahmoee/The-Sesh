# The Sesh Privacy Policy

**Effective and last updated: August 8, 2026**

The Sesh is an adults-only lifestyle journal and optional social community for people of legal age in places where cannabis is legal. This policy explains how Sowens Studios ("we") handles information in The Sesh. The app is not medical advice and does not sell cannabis or facilitate purchases.

## Information stored on your device and iCloud

The app stores profile settings, journal entries, session details, moods, effects, notes, stash and product records, goals, insights, playlists, preferences, and photos you choose to add. Local session credentials are stored in Apple Keychain. You may enable private iCloud synchronization for supported journal and profile data; Apple processes that data under its own terms.

Camera or photo-library access is used only when you choose an image. Notification access is used for reminders, community activity, and other alerts you enable. Apple Intelligence features described as on-device remain on supported devices unless the app clearly identifies a network feature.

## Accounts and community features

You may use Sign in with Apple or a device-scoped guest identity. When you connect to community features, our Cloudflare-hosted service may process:

- your Apple account subject identifier and, if Apple provides it, relay or account email;
- profile name, handle, friend code, device identifier, session token, DeviceCheck result, and push token;
- friends, blocks, follows, presence, sharing preferences, reports, and moderation records;
- statuses, strain or session details you elect to share, now-playing information, posts, comments, reactions, polls, messages, and uploaded community images; and
- technical request information such as IP address, timestamps, endpoint, response status, and security/rate-limit data generated by Cloudflare.

Privacy controls determine whether friends can see activity, strain details, music, session duration, live status, and friend-code discoverability. Public or shared content can be seen, saved, or reshared by its audience. Do not post information you want to remain private.

## Spotify and music services

If you connect Spotify, authorization and refresh tokens are processed to provide now-playing, search, playlist, and export features. Spotify processes account and playback information under its own privacy policy. Apple Music features are governed by Apple's terms and permissions. Disconnect a music service to stop future access.

## How information is used

We use information to authenticate users, sync requested features, operate friends and community spaces, deliver notifications, prevent abuse, investigate reports, maintain security, and provide support. We do not use third-party advertising SDKs, sell personal information, or use community information for cross-app tracking.

## Retention and deletion

On-device data remains until you remove it or delete the app. iCloud data remains until removed through the app or your Apple account controls. Community account and content records are retained while needed to provide the service, protect users, resolve reports, meet legal obligations, and maintain security. Some records may remain in backups or abuse-prevention logs for a limited period.

You may sign out and remove local information in the app. To delete server-held account information and associated community content, email [support@sowensstudios.com](mailto:support@sowensstudios.com) from the address associated with Sign in with Apple or include your handle and friend code so we can verify the request. We may retain limited records where required for safety, fraud prevention, or law.

## Safety, age, and sensitive information

The Sesh is for adults age 21 or the legal age where they live and is not directed to children. Cannabis-related journal and social information can be sensitive. Choose sharing settings carefully and do not use the app while driving or operating machinery. If we learn that a minor submitted personal information, we will take reasonable steps to delete it.

## Security and international processing

We use platform security, HTTPS, signed sessions, rate limits, DeviceCheck where configured, and access controls. No system is completely secure. Cloudflare, Apple, Spotify, and other providers may process information in countries other than yours under their own terms.

## Changes and contact

We may update this policy as features change. Material updates will be identified by a new date and, when appropriate, an in-app notice. Questions and deletion requests: [support@sowensstudios.com](mailto:support@sowensstudios.com).
